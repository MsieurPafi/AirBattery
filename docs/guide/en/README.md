# AirBattery Plus user guide

AirBattery Plus shows the battery of your Mac and of the devices around it in the menu bar, the Dock and widgets.

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): install, first launch, permissions.
2. [Supported devices](devices.md): what can be read, and how.
3. [Using the app](using-the-app.md): menu bar, Dock, widgets, battery alerts, pinned and hidden devices.
4. [Nearcast](nearcast.md): see the batteries of your other Macs.
5. [Command line](command-line.md): the `abtp` tool.
6. [Settings](settings.md): every option, tab by tab.
7. [Permissions and privacy](permissions-privacy.md): what the app asks for, and what stays on your Mac.
8. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): a device is missing, an iPhone or Watch doesn't show up, and more.

Changes from one version to the next are listed in the [changelog](../../../CHANGELOG.md).
