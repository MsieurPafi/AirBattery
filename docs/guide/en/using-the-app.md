# Using the app

[Guide](README.md)

## Where it shows

Choose in **Settings › General › Show AirBattery Plus**:

- **Menu Bar** (default): click the icon to see all your devices.
- **Dock**: the Dock icon shows your devices' batteries. Click it to see the full list. Showing the app in the Dock uses a little more power than the menu bar or widgets.
- **Both**, or **None** (widgets only; open the app again from Applications to reach its settings).

The menu bar icon can show this Mac's battery, like the system's own battery icon (**Dynamic Battery Icon**, in **Settings › Menu Bar & Dock**).

The buttons at the top of the list: ⓧ quits the app, ⓘ shows its version, ⚙︎ opens the settings. With [Nearcast](nearcast.md) on, a fourth one refreshes the other Macs.

## Device actions

Hover over a device in the list to see when it last reported and its actions:

- 🔔 **Battery alert**: get a notification when the device goes below a level (20% by default) or charges above one (80% by default). Click the bell again to change the levels, turn each alert on or off, or delete the alert. A notification can be snoozed for 30 minutes.
- 📌 **Pin**: the device gets its own icon in the menu bar, with its battery.
- 📋 **Copy**: copies the device's name, for example to add it to the blocklist.
- 👁 **Hide**: the device moves to the hidden section at the bottom of the list. Click it there to show it again.

### Hide a device

Hidden devices are kept in **Settings › Blocklist**. To show only some devices instead, turn on **Allowlist Mode** there and add their names.

## Order of the list

**Settings › Menu Bar & Dock › Sort Devices**: by name, lowest battery first or highest battery first. It also applies to the Dock icon and the widgets. AirPods stay grouped with their case, an Apple Watch with its iPhone, an Apple Pencil with its iPad.

Devices that stop reporting are removed after 20 minutes (**Remove Offline Device**).

## Widgets

Add them from the macOS widget gallery (search for AirBattery Plus). Several widgets are available in small, medium and large sizes: lists of devices with or without percentages, and a widget for a single device. To choose its device, right-click the widget › **Edit Widget** and enter the device's name, as shown in the list (on macOS 12 and 13: **Settings › Widget › Single Device Widget**).

Widgets only update while the app is running. If one says AirBattery Plus is not running, launch the app.
